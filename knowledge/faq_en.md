# Acme Burgers hiring FAQ (English)

## How much does a crew member earn?
<!-- id: pay-crew -->
Crew members (front counter and service) earn a gross hourly wage that depends on the country. In Spain the range is 9.80–11.00 EUR per hour. In Mexico it is 55–70 MXN per hour. Your exact rate depends on your experience and the shifts you work, and the recruiter confirms it in the offer.

## How much does a kitchen team member earn?
<!-- id: pay-kitchen -->
Kitchen team members (grill, fryer and preparation) earn a gross hourly wage that depends on the country. In Spain the range is 10.20–12.00 EUR per hour. In Mexico it is 60–75 MXN per hour. The final rate depends on experience and is confirmed in the written offer.

## How much does a rider earn?
<!-- id: pay-rider -->
Riders (delivery) earn a gross hourly wage that depends on the country. In Spain the range is 10.50–12.50 EUR per hour. In Mexico it is 65–85 MXN per hour. Riders can also receive a small bonus per delivery in busy periods. The offer states your exact rate.

## What contract types are available?
<!-- id: contract-types -->
Acme Burgers offers part-time and full-time contracts, and fixed-term contracts for seasonal peaks. Most new team members start with a fixed-term or part-time contract, and many move to a permanent one after a few months. In Mexico the contract follows local labour law and includes social security. The recruiter explains the contract before you sign.

## What are the shifts and their hours?
<!-- id: shifts -->
There are three shift windows. Morning shifts start before 12:00, afternoon shifts run from 12:00 to 18:00, and night shifts start from 18:00. Stores are open until late, so night shifts are common on weekends. You tell us which windows you can work, and we try to match your availability when we build the rota.

## How many hours per week will I work?
<!-- id: weekly-hours -->
Contracts range from 10 to 40 hours per week. Part-time roles usually have between 10 and 25 hours, and full-time roles are close to 40. You choose the number of hours you want during this screening, and the store manager confirms what is available. Schedules are published at least a week in advance.

## Do I get a uniform?
<!-- id: uniform -->
Yes. Acme Burgers gives you a uniform for free: two shirts or tops, a cap or hair net, and an apron for kitchen roles. You only need to bring black trousers and non-slip closed shoes. You wash and look after the uniform at home. Replacement items are free when worn out.

## Are meals included?
<!-- id: meals -->
Yes. You get a free meal during every shift longer than four hours, chosen from the staff menu. You also get a discount on food when you visit the store on your days off. Drinks during the shift are free. Special diets, such as vegetarian or gluten-free, can be accommodated; just tell your manager.

## Is there training?
<!-- id: training -->
Yes. Every new team member completes a paid onboarding of about two days: food safety, hygiene, the till or kitchen stations, and customer service. A buddy from the team helps you during your first shifts. No previous experience is needed. Food safety training is provided by Acme Burgers at no cost to you.

## Can I grow my career at Acme Burgers?
<!-- id: career -->
Yes. A typical path is crew member or kitchen team member, then shift leader, then assistant manager, and finally store manager. Many managers started as crew. Promotions depend on performance, reliability and completed training modules. You can discuss your goals with your manager at the regular review, held every few months.

## Do riders need their own vehicle?
<!-- id: rider-vehicle -->
Riders can use a moped, a motorbike or a car. Acme Burgers does not provide the vehicle, so having your own helps; if you do not have one, tell us and a recruiter will look at the options with you. You receive a monthly allowance toward fuel and maintenance, and a delivery bag is provided.

## What licence and insurance do riders need?
<!-- id: rider-licence -->
Riders need a valid driving licence for their vehicle type. The vehicle must have valid insurance that covers delivery work, and it must pass the mandatory technical inspection where applicable. Bring the licence and insurance documents to the in-store trial. Acme Burgers does not pay your personal vehicle insurance.

## Do I keep tips?
<!-- id: tips -->
Yes. Tips left by customers, in cash or by card, belong to the team and are shared fairly among the people on shift. Riders keep the tips they receive on deliveries. Tips are not part of your hourly wage and are never counted against it. Your manager explains how tips are shared in your store.

## What are the steps of the hiring process?
<!-- id: hiring-process -->
There are four steps. First, this voice screening, where we collect your basic details. Second, a recruiter reviews your application. Third, an in-store trial shift where you meet the team. Fourth, an offer. A human recruiter makes every hiring decision; this assistant only gathers information and cannot approve or reject anyone.

## How long does the hiring process take?
<!-- id: hiring-timeline -->
The voice screening takes about five minutes. A recruiter usually reviews your application within three business days and contacts you to arrange the in-store trial. The trial takes place within about a week after that. The offer follows within two business days of the trial. In busy periods it can take a few days longer.

## What documents do I need on my first day?
<!-- id: documents -->
Bring a valid ID card or passport, proof of your right to work, and your bank account details for payroll. In Spain, also your social security number. In Mexico, also your CURP and RFC. Riders must bring their driving licence and vehicle insurance. Your recruiter sends you the complete list before your start date.

## Where are the Acme Burgers stores?
<!-- id: store-locations -->
Acme Burgers has stores in Madrid, Barcelona and Valencia in Spain, and in Mexico City and Guadalajara in Mexico. The assistant can tell you which stores are in your city. The full list, with addresses and opening hours, is on https://careers.acme-burgers.example. You choose your preferred store during the screening.

## Are the stores accessible?
<!-- id: accessibility -->
Our stores aim to be accessible to everyone. Most have step-free entrances and adapted restrooms, though this varies by building. If you need adjustments, such as a particular schedule, tell the recruiter and we will look for reasonable arrangements. Applicants with disabilities are welcome, and we assess everyone on the same criteria.

## What does the voice screening record?
<!-- id: privacy-recorded -->
With your consent, the assistant keeps a transcript of the call and a record of your details for the recruiter to review. No audio is stored. The record holds data such as contact details, role, store, availability, start date, eligibility yes/no answers and experience. The tone of the conversation is scored so Ana can offer a recruiter if you seem frustrated. If you do not consent, you can contact a recruiter instead.

## How can I ask for my data to be deleted?
<!-- id: privacy-deletion -->
You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data. Write to privacidad@acme-burgers.example with your name and phone number, and we will confirm the deletion within 30 days. The transcript and your record are kept for a limited retention period (30 days by default) and then deleted automatically. Asking for deletion does not affect any future application.

## How can I contact a recruiter?
<!-- id: contact-recruiter -->
You can reach the recruiting team by email at reclutamiento@acme-burgers.example, or through the careers page at https://careers.acme-burgers.example. Include your name and the city where you want to work. The team replies within two business days. If you prefer, tell the assistant and it will pass a note to a recruiter.

## What is the minimum age?
<!-- id: minimum-age -->
All roles require being 18 or over. This applies to crew, kitchen and rider positions in both Spain and Mexico. During the screening we only ask whether you are 18 or over. A recruiter reviews every application; if you are under 18, you are welcome to apply again when you turn 18.

## Do I need the right to work?
<!-- id: right-to-work -->
Yes. You need the legal right to work in the country where the store is. In Spain, citizens of the EU and EEA can work freely; others need a valid work permit. In Mexico, foreign applicants need a valid work permit. We ask you to confirm this during the screening, and the recruiter verifies your documents before the offer.

## When can I start?
<!-- id: start-date -->
Start dates depend on the store and on how fast the process goes. Most new team members start between one and three weeks after accepting the offer. If you have a notice period at another job, tell us your earliest start date during the screening. Start dates must be in the future and within the next three months.

## Can I switch to another store later?
<!-- id: switching-stores -->
Yes. After your probation period, you can ask your manager for a transfer to another store, in the same city or a different one, depending on openings. Transfers keep your seniority. Moving between countries needs a new contract and right to work. Speak to your manager as early as possible so they can plan.

## Can I work while studying or with a flexible schedule?
<!-- id: students-flexible -->
Yes. Many team members are students. Part-time contracts of 10 to 20 hours are common, and you can tell us in advance about exams or classes. Availability is gathered during the screening, and managers publish the rota at least a week ahead. Swapping shifts with a teammate is possible with the manager's approval.
